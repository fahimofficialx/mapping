⚡ Connect to Pocket Option

Enter your Pocket Option session credentials to receive live WebSocket data from m.pocketoption.com. Without credentials, the analyzer runs in demo/simulation mode.

1
Open m.pocketoption.com and log in to your account.
2
Open DevTools (F12), go to Application → Cookies → pocketoption.com. Copy the value of PHPSESSID or ssid.
3
Alternatively: in the Network tab, filter by WS, click the WebSocket connection, and look for the auth message to find the session token.
4
Paste below and click Connect.
This is stored locally and never transmitted except to Pocket Option's own WebSocket.
